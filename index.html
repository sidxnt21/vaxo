How it started

The Story of Hile Machha

Founded in Kathmandu by passionate aquatic hobbyists, Hile Machha (literally translating to "Mud Fish", the local Nepalese name for native snakeheads) began as a private effort to source healthy, exotic Channa species.

Frustrated with misidentified fish, poor conditioning, and high loss rates in typical pet shops, we built a premium, direct-to-hobbyist online platform. Today, we specialize exclusively in the genus Channa, bridging the gap between world-class international breeders and Nepalese collectors.

We focus entirely on importing, quarantining, and safely distributing premium Snakehead varieties across Nepal, while promoting responsible keeping practices for these highly predatory and intelligent creatures.

Our Mission

To supply premium, healthy, and certified Channa species to aquarium enthusiasts across Nepal, ensuring professional-grade packaging and direct expert care support for every purchase.

Our Vision

To foster a vibrant, responsible, and knowledgeable Snakehead hobbyist community in Nepal, setting the standard for live fish e-commerce, quarantine protocols, and aquatic biotope simulation.

Our Commitment to Fish Quality

Every fish is treated as a prize specimen. Before any sale, our stock goes through a minimum of 14 days of quarantine. They are dewormed, transitioned to commercial feeding, and carefully inspected for spinal straightness, scale health, and fin development. We guarantee that your Channa will arrive healthy, active, and parasite-free.

Generally, Channa are apex predators and territorial. Dwarf species (like Andrao or Bleheri) can sometimes be kept in pairs if plenty of hiding spaces are provided, but they should not be kept with small fish like tetras, which will become food. Large species like Barca or Marulioides are best kept in solo specimen displays or with robust tankmates like large catfish or plecos in massive aquariums.
